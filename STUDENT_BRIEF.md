# Proof-of-work video rules

Only the **team lead** submits, once, for the whole team, through the **Google Form** linked from Classroom. Do not submit a Classroom file, a Classroom link, or email.

- Every video must be at least **30 seconds**.
- Set each video to **Unlisted** (not private, not public).
- Paste the YouTube **Share** link (`youtu.be/…` or `youtube.com/watch?v=…`) and the **11-character video ID**. Do not paste Studio, Shorts, playlist, or channel URLs.
- The team lead’s name, or ID and name, must be visible on the **side of each breadboard** (paper tape is fine).
- After the deadline: do not use YouTube Studio Editor (no trim, cut, blur, or audio replace), do not change privacy, do not delete the video, and do not replace the Form link with a different video.

**Late work:** the late submission policy starts as soon as the deadline passes. It applies to the **entire team**, not only the team lead.

Staff snapshot the submitted URL shortly after the deadline and later check whether observable properties of that same YouTube video have changed.
