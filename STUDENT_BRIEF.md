# Proof-of-work video rules

Submit an **unlisted** YouTube video through Google Classroom.

- Do not set the video to private or public.
- Do not delete the video.
- Do not use YouTube Studio Editor after the deadline (no trim, cut, blur, or audio replace).
- Do not replace the Classroom link with a different video.

Staff will snapshot the submitted video shortly after the deadline and check later whether observable properties of that same YouTube URL have changed.
